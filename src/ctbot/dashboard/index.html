        Autopilot

        Autopilot trades the practice account for you. It picks from every strategy in the library below: trades that last a few hours, overnight holds, trades that last a few days, and long-term funds. Before each short-term buy, Claude (the AI) looks at the idea, says yes or no, and picks a safety sell point: the price where the bot sells to stop the loss (a stop-loss). The bot decides how much to buy and enforces every safety limit. Winners have no fixed sell price: when the price goes up, the safety sell point moves up with it, and the bot sells when the price falls back to it. Strategies that failed testing, or lost money over their last 10+ trades, are benched: they aren't allowed to trade.
